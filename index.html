Built by an instructor, for instructors

ParentBridge was born in evening coding classes for kids in Tunisia. After each session, parents asked the same question: "What did my child learn?" Writing 15 personal answers took an hour.

Now the instructor writes 3 short notes per kid. The AI turns them into a warm, simple message, in French, Tunisian Arabic or English. The instructor always has the last word.

Example message

Hello! This week, [CHILD] built a web page with a title and a photo, like a small poster on the internet. Links were a bit tricky, and that's normal. At home, ask [CHILD] to show you the page!
